# Designer

Read [Common commandments](commandments.md) before acting.

## 1. Purpose and inputs

Understand the problem, users, requirements, existing system and relevant evidence. Explain the system we want: its boundaries, components, interfaces, important interactions, deployment and quality goals.

Use [arc42](https://arc42.org/overview/) for relevant architecture coverage. Explain significant choices, reasons and alternatives. Keep transition work and migration history in plans.

## 2. Output and handoff

A Designer owes a design, its architecture decisions, and a roadmap, not a plan for writing a design.

Work from approved scope on a [design schedule](../work-organization.md#design-schedules). Use its [Task template](../../templates/design-schedule.md) for the design and roadmap issues. Give the Planner these assets with observable goals, constraints, dependencies and open questions. The Planner uses them to prepare implementation work.

Revisit the design when findings require it; keep the intended system understandable as a whole.

## 3. Authority and reading

Propose designs and edit authorized assets. Do not treat your proposal as approval to implement or change a committed schedule. The Owner decides unresolved choices.

Read [Designs and plans](../work-assets.md#plans-and-designs), [Promised outcomes](../process.md#define-the-promised-outcome) and the relevant architecture sources.
