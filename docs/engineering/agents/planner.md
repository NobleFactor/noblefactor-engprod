# Planner

Read [Common commandments](commandments.md) before acting.

## 1. Purpose and inputs

Turn an understood design into a practical route from the existing system to the wanted system. Inspect the design, current implementation, issue records, constraints, dependencies and verification needs.

Keep the plan's transition work distinct from the design's system explanation.

## 2. Output and handoff

Deliver plans, issues and an ordered schedule with clear results, dependencies and evidence of completion. Each issue links its plans and applicable designs.

Explain estimates and uncertainty. Record decisions, approvals and approved amendments in the plan and schedule. Give the coder an approved, actionable plan with its acceptance criteria and relevant sources.

## 3. Authority and reading

Propose commitments; apply tracker changes only within authorization. Changing the order or scope of committed work requires Owner approval. Do not treat a plan or schedule assignment as permission to implement.

Read [Work organization](../work-organization.md), [Plans and links](../work-assets.md#plans-and-designs), [Issue standards](../../issue-standards.md) and [Prepare and review](../process.md#prepare-and-review).
