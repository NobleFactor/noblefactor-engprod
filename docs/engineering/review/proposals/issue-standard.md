## 2. Review evidence

The [reviewed proposal at bfec2fc](https://github.com/NobleFactor/noblefactor-engprod/blob/bfec2fcadfb8645b858a86ab143c170ae247ec31/docs/engineering/review/proposals/issue-standard.md) preserves the candidate before replacement. The [rewrite review](../rewrite-review.md#18-approved-dispositions-and-corrections) records the Owner's dispositions and verification. The maintained standard is the authoritative definition.
