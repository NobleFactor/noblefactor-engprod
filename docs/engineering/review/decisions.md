# Decisions established in review

These records preserve the Owner's decisions and their approval evidence. The [decision-record standard](../../standards/documentation.md#decision-records) defines their structure; current requirements remain in the handbook pages each decision governs.

Entries 1–58 retain their earlier decision text. The [pre-format log](sources/rewrite-2026-11-19/handbook-decisions-before-format.md.txt) preserves the original layout and source wording. Missing alternatives, proposers and approval times are not inferred. A known approval date remains a date; recording timestamps and recorder attribution identify this formatting pass.

Accepted describes approval of the recorded choice at that time. Some earlier choices are superseded wholly or in part; their explanations retain that scope. Consult current handbook guidance and linked later decisions before treating a historical statement as a current instruction.

<a id="decision-59"></a>

## 59. Structure decision records with a headline and YAML attribution

Use a headline, decision explanation and a fenced YAML attribution block for each handbook or architecture decision record. The [documentation standard](../../standards/documentation.md#decision-records) defines the format; the [shared template](../../templates/decision.md) demonstrates it.

The explanation records scope, context, reasons, considered alternatives, consequences and available evidence. The footer identifies status, attribution and distinct decision/recording times. Preserve stable identifiers and source precision; record changed outcomes through linked decisions.

Plain prose attribution is easy to read but harder to validate consistently. File frontmatter can describe a whole document, while a footer identifies each decision within a shared log. A fenced YAML footer keeps the decision headline first and makes attribution visible. Automated consumers must recognize and validate the footer explicitly.

[Arc42's decision content](https://docs.arc42.org/section-9/), [rejected-alternative guidance](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/9-6/) and [timestamp guidance](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/9-8/), together with [Google's descriptive-heading guidance](https://developers.google.com/style/headings), support the content and presentation. The footer's exact layout is the Owner-approved local convention, not a layout prescribed by either reference. David Noble approved implementation in this conversation on 2026-11-19: “Make the changes,” followed by “Add that documentation.” The [plan amendment](../../plans/doc/581-schedule-command-consumers.md#712-structured-decision-records) records the scope.
