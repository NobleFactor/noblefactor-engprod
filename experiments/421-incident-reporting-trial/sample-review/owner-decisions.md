# Owner decisions on the sample

## Replay ordering

The Owner accepted the proposed simulated sequence for the trial: eligible earlier source positions may precede a case when their date range ends on or before its start date. Equal-date source order is a simulation, not established historical chronology. Results must not be presented as proof of what the agent knew at the time of the incident.

This decision does not approve the cases, pattern anchors, reference answers, or full sample.

## C01

The Owner agreed that the incident in which the agent falsely claimed the Owner ran `/add-dir` should be grouped with earlier incidents of stating unverified claims as fact. The proposed anchor is P003. This approval applies to that grouping, not to other proposed cases or the full sample.

## C02

The Owner agreed that continuing to use short command options after being told to stop belongs to the same recurring pattern as earlier use of short options. The proposed anchor for that grouping is P006. This decision does not establish whether other patterns also apply or approve other cases.
